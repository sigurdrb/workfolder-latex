\section{Introduction}
Dynamical model of CAS system.

Formula for flow vs speed:
$Q = K_1 \cdot H \cdot n$, where n is the impeller speed in RPM, and Q is $m^3/h$
For the SR2000 pump the area of operation is between 40-130 rpm.
If we linearize around the rated point and switch to units $m^3/s$, we get: \newline

$\dfrac{20000}{60^2} =K_1 \cdot 130 \Longrightarrow K_1 \approx 0.042735$. \newline

The flow out of the pen is designed so we have an exchange rate of 35 minute for the entire volume of the pen which is $20 000m^3$.
If we switch units to $m^3/h$ and linearize around 0.06m, we get: \newline
\begin{align}  
K_{qo} = \dfrac{20 000m^3}{35\cdot (60s)} \approx 9.5238m^3/s\\
q_o = \dfrac{K_{qo}}{0.06} \cdot x_1
\end{align}
where $x_1$ is the height difference inside-outside the pen.
C is the capacitance of the tank \newline
\begin{align}
C&=\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial h} = \pi r^2 = \pi \cdot 25.25m^2\\
&\approx  1288.25m^2
\end{align}

\begin{align}
    C \dot{x_1} &= q_i - q_o\\
    &= K_1 \cdot u_1 - \dfrac{K_{qo}}{0.06}x_1\\
    \dot{x_1}&= -\dfrac{K_{qo}}{0.06\cdot C}x_1 + \dfrac{K_1}{C}u_1 \label{eq:flow}
\end{align}

\subsection{Oxygen model}

\begin{align}
    \dot{a} &= c_\text{in}\cdot r_\text{in} - c_\text{out}\cdot r_\text{out}
\end{align}

Where $a$ is the amount of oxygen in the system, $c_\text{in} \left[ \frac{\text{kg}}{\text{m}^3} \right] $ is the concentration going in, 
$c_\text{out}$ is the concentration going out. $r_\text{in}\left[ \frac{\text{m}^3}{h} \right]$ is the rate of the amount going in and 
$r_\text{in}$ is the rate of oksygen going out.
Substituting this with the state variables the equation becomes when linearized around 0.06cm
\begin{equation} \label{eq:ox_simp}
    \dot{x_2} =  +K_2(u_1+u_2+u_3) + x_3 -K_4-K_{qo}\cdot \dfrac{x_2}{20000}
\end{equation}

where F(s) is the transfer function between
We also have an additional contribution to $x_2$ from the oxygenation system, which is on the from

\begin{align*}
    \dfrac{X_2}{U_4} &= \dfrac{K_3}{1+T_1s}-K_{qo}\cdot \dfrac{x_2}{20000}
\end{align*}


Where $K_2$ is the amount of oxygen inside the sea water (concentration times flow). $u_1$, $u_2$, $u_3$ are the control signals to the inlet pump (in RPM). 
$u_4$, $u_5$, $u_6$ is the control signal to the control valve for the oxygenation system.
$G_1(s)$ is the transfer function from the control signal to the valve to the outout flow
$K_4$ is the assumed constant consumption of oxygen from the fish.\newline

\begin{align} \label{eq:valve}
    G_1(s)&=\dfrac{K_5}{1+T_1s}
\end{align}

$K_5$

The constant $K_2$ is relating the pump speed to rate of oxygen that is inserted in the pen $\frac{g}{h}$.

Normal $O_2$ levels in sea water is $\frac{7g}{m^3}$.

From equation \ref{eq:flow} we have the relationship between RPM and oxygen into the CAS (with assumption of constant oxygen concentration of $\frac{7g}{m^3}$). \newline
\begin{equation}
    \dfrac{x_2}{u_1} = K_2 =  7\frac{g}{m^3} \cdot K_1 \approx 0.299145\frac{g}{s}
\end{equation}



Maximum $o_2$ injection pr. inlet branch is specified to be $104.2kg/h$ ($\approx 29g/s$)
If we assume a linear relationship between the control signal and outlet flow, where the maximum oxygen flow is $\approx 29g/s$ pr. specification, we get:

\begin{equation}
    \dfrac{x_2}{u_4} = K_5 =\frac{1}{100}\cdot 29\frac{g}{s} = 2.9\frac{g}{s}
\end{equation}
where $u_4$ is a control signal between 0-100\%.\newline

The time constant for the Burkert 2865 valve is given to  $<20\mathrm{ms}$. Whit the possibility to introduce a ramp delay between 0..10s \newline

Equation \ref{eq:valve} then becomes
\begin{equation}
    G_1(s) = \dfrac{2.9}{1+0.02s}
\end{equation}

The average consumption of fish in this model is $500mg O_2/kgh$. 
If we switch units to seconds and gram, and assume a total of 1 000 000kg salmon we get the value of $K_4$ to be \newline
$500 \cdot \dfrac{1000000}{60^2\cdot 1000}\frac{g O_2}{s} \approx 139 g O_2/s $

\begin{align*}
    x_1 &= \text{inner water height difference}                         & u_1&= \text{pump 1 speed [rpm]}\\
    x_2 &= \text{amount of oxygen inside the pen}                       & u_2&= \text{pump 2 speed [rpm]}\\

\end{align*}

\begin{align*}
